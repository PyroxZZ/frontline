Choose your country

Everyone is at war with everyone. Hold an enemy capital for 20 seconds and that country capitulates to you.

Tutorial

About five minutes, played in a real game as France. The other countries stay asleep while you learn:

  • moving the camera and selecting divisions
  • attacking provinces and widening an attack
  • stances and holding the front
  • recruiting, supply and how a war is won

Multiplayer

Peer to peer, up to four players - no server, no account. Empty seats are played by the AI.
